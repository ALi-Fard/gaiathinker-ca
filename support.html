District Procurement & DPA Requests

School districts in British Columbia may require a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) support as part of their procurement approval process. GaiaThinker is designed to meet these requirements. To initiate a DPA request, email us with "DPA Request" in the subject line and we will respond within two business days.
