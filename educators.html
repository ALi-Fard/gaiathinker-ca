Platform Features
Everything you need. Nothing you don't.
Designed with teachers, not for IT departments.
BC Climate Data
Real emissions data, provincial policy, and local ecosystem information — not global abstractions. Students explore the province they live in.
Professor Ember AI Tutor
Socratic AI that never gives answers — it asks the next right question. Pushes students to evaluate evidence and build reasoning independently.
Gamified Engagement
Streak mechanics, scenario classification, and a timed civic briefing — designed to hold Gen Z attention through an entire 75-minute lesson.
No Student Accounts
Students access through an educator-initiated session. No registration, no email, no parental consent form. FIPPA-compliant from the first click.
Formative Assessment
Aggregated, anonymized progress summaries for the class — not individual surveillance. See which concepts need reinforcement without student data.
First Peoples Principles
Learning design integrates BC's First Peoples Principles of Learning and Indigenous land relationships — woven into the curriculum, not added on.
