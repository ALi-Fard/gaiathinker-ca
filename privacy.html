Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  GaiaLink Intelligence Systems Inc.

Plain language summary: GaiaThinker does not create student accounts, does not collect student names or emails, and runs on Microsoft Azure with Canadian data residency. No personal student data leaves Canada.

1. Who We Are

GaiaThinker is operated by GaiaLink Intelligence Systems Inc., a British Columbia corporation. GaiaThinker is an AI-powered climate literacy platform designed for Grades 9–12 schools across British Columbia.

We can be reached at: ali@gaiasystems.ca

2. No Student Accounts Required

FIPPA Key Requirement

GaiaThinker does not create, store, or process individual student accounts. Students access the platform through an educator-initiated session. No student registration, login, name, or email address is required or collected at any point during student use.

3. What Data We Collect

GaiaThinker collects only the minimum data required to provide the service:

  • Session interaction data — which lesson cards were viewed, time on task, game responses, and scenario classifications. This data is aggregated and used solely to generate educator progress summaries.
  • AI conversation content — text entered into the Professor Ember AI tutor during a session. This is processed in real time by Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service and is not retained after the session ends.

We do not collect: student names, email addresses, birth dates, device identifiers, IP addresses tied to individuals, photographs, biometric data, or any data not listed above.

4. Educator and Institutional Data

Educators and school administrators who contact GaiaThinker directly for pilot access provide:

  • Name and professional title
  • School or district name
  • Professional email address

This information is used solely to manage pilot participation, provide onboarding support, and communicate updates about the platform. It is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for marketing purposes beyond GaiaThinker communications.

5. Canadian Data Residency

Azure Canada

GaiaThinker's AI components are powered by Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service hosted in Canadian Azure regions. Student interaction data processed through Professor Ember does not leave Canada.

GaiaLink Intelligence Systems Inc. is committed to ensuring all data infrastructure remains within Canadian jurisdiction throughout the platform's operation.

6. FIPPA and PIPEDA Compliance

GaiaThinker is designed to meet the requirements of:

  • BC Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA) — the primary privacy legislation governing BC public school operations
  • Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) — federal private-sector privacy law

School districts procuring or evaluating GaiaThinker may request a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) support document by contacting us directly.

7. Microsoft Responsible AI

Responsible AI

GaiaThinker's AI tutor (Professor Ember) is built on Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service and operates in accordance with Microsoft's Responsible AI principles: fairness, reliability, safety, privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability.

Professor Ember does not make decisions about individual students, does not assign grades or assessments, and does not retain conversation history between separate sessions. The AI is designed to support human educator judgment, not replace it.

8. First Peoples and Indigenous Data Sovereignty

GaiaThinker is designed with awareness of the OCAP® principles (Ownership, Control, Access, Possession) as they relate to Indigenous data sovereignty in British Columbia. No data relating to Indigenous students is collected beyond the aggregated session data described in Section 3. GaiaThinker does not profile, segment, or identify Indigenous students in any way.

9. Cookies and Tracking

GaiaThinker uses session-only cookies strictly necessary for platform functionality (e.g., maintaining lesson state during a session). We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or analytics that identify individual users. No third-party advertising or tracking scripts are loaded by the platform.

10. Data Retention

  • AI conversation content: not retained after session ends
  • Aggregated session interaction data: retained for the duration of the pilot program, then deleted or anonymized
  • Educator contact information: retained while the professional relationship is active, deleted upon request

11. Your Rights

Under FIPPA and PIPEDA, individuals have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ali.reza.farshadfard@gaiasystems.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

12. Accessibility

GaiaThinker is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards, consistent with Microsoft's accessibility commitments and BC's accessibility requirements for publicly deployed digital tools.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be communicated to participating institutions by email before taking effect. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
