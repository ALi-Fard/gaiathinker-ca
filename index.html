The Problem

Climate curriculum is failing the students who need it most.

Most climate education presents the crisis without the agency. Students absorb statistics about a problem they didn't create and can't yet influence — producing anxiety, not understanding.

British Columbia's net-zero commitments, clean energy targets, and land-use decisions will be made by the generation sitting in today's classrooms. They are not being prepared.

The Solution

From passive learners to climate decision-makers.

GaiaThinker replaces abstract curriculum with British Columbia's own climate data, positions students as policymakers, and uses AI to build the critical thinking that persists long after the lesson ends.

01 — Investigate
Real BC Climate Data
Students explore British Columbia's actual emissions sources, local climate impacts, and provincial policy decisions — grounded in the places and ecosystems they know. Not global abstractions.
02 — Decide
Gamified Civic Action
Students classify climate scenarios, build streaks, and brief a simulated provincial body — moving from passive observer to active decision-maker. Every session ends with a recommendation, not a summary.
03 — Think
Professor Ember — AI Tutor
Powered by Azure OpenAI. Ember uses Socratic questioning — never giving answers, always building reasoning. Students are challenged to evaluate evidence before conclusions are confirmed.
How It Works

One session. Three phases. Lasting understanding.

Phase 01
Climate Explorer
Students work through cause, impact, and solution cards tied to British Columbia's real emissions data. Each card surfaces a local decision point, not a global abstraction.
Science & Social Studies
Phase 02
The Civic Briefing
Students classify live BC scenarios and brief a simulated provincial body under time pressure. Instant feedback. Streak mechanics. Real stakes.
Critical Thinking
Phase 03
AI Dialogue with Ember
Professor Ember engages each student in directed Socratic conversation. No answers given — only the right question at the right moment. FIPPA-compliant by design.
