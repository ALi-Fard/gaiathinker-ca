About

We believe the next generation deserves better tools.

GaiaLink Intelligence Systems is a British Columbia company building AI-powered education for the climate era.

Our Mission
Turn climate anxiety into civic competence.

BC's Gen Z students are deeply aware of the climate crisis — and deeply underprepared to engage with it. They absorb headlines about a problem they didn't create and can't yet influence. The result is anxiety without agency.

GaiaThinker flips that. We take British Columbia's actual climate data — real emissions, real land use, real policy decisions — and put students in the role of decision-maker, not spectator. Every session ends with a recommendation, not a summary.

Our AI tutor, Professor Ember, never gives answers. It asks the right question at the right moment, pushing students to build reasoning that outlasts the lesson.

The Company
GaiaLink Intelligence Systems Inc.

Founded in British Columbia, GaiaLink Intelligence Systems builds AI tools that serve the public interest — starting with climate education for the province that will need it most.

We are a Microsoft Azure partner, building on Azure OpenAI Service with Canadian data residency. Our work is aligned with Microsoft's Responsible AI principles: fairness, reliability, safety, privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability.

We are designed for BC public school procurement — FIPPA-aligned, no student accounts, no personal data collected.
