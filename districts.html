Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Process

Most BC school districts require a DPA or Privacy Impact Assessment support as part of procurement. GaiaThinker is designed to support this process. Here's how it works:

Step 01
Submit DPA request
Email us with "DPA Request" in the subject. Include district name and privacy officer contact.
Step 02
Receive draft DPA
Within two business days we provide a draft DPA and PIA support document for your district's review.
Step 03
Review & execute
Your district's privacy officer reviews and signs. We accommodate district-specific requirements.
